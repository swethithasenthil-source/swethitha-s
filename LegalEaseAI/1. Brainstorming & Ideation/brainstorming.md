# 1. Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project Name
LegalEaseAI

## Problem Identified

Many people find legal documents difficult to understand because
legal language can be complex and difficult for non-lawyers.

Users may struggle to:
- Understand legal terminology
- Identify important clauses
- Summarize lengthy legal documents
- Find important information quickly
- Understand the general meaning of a document

## Proposed Solution

LegalEaseAI is an AI-powered application designed to help users
understand legal documents using simple and understandable language.

## Main Idea

The system allows a user to upload or provide legal document content.
The application processes the document and generates an easy-to-understand
summary and explanation.

## Brainstormed Features

1. Legal document summarization
2. Simple-language explanation
3. Important clause identification
4. AI-based question answering
5. Document generation
6. Downloadable documents
7. User-friendly interface

## Target Users

- Students
- Individuals
- Small businesses
- General users
- People who need basic understanding of legal documents

## Expected Outcome

A simple AI-based platform that makes legal information easier to
understand without requiring the user to read complicated legal language.

## Technology Ideas

- Python
- FastAPI
- Streamlit/Frontend
- Google Gemini API
- HTML/CSS
- Document processing libraries
